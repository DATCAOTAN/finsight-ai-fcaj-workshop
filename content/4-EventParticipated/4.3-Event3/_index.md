# Event Report "FCAJ - 3-DAY AGENTFORCE WORKSHOP AGENDA"

**Date:** Saturday, 01/08/2026  
**Location:** Floor 26, Bitexco Financial Tower  
**Role:** Attendee & Lab Participant  

### Event Objectives

- Provide a comprehensive introduction to Agentic AI in the enterprise landscape.
- Guide participants on building and deploying an AI Agent system from scratch using **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore** and the **Strands Agents SDK**.
- Experience **Vibe Coding with Kiro**, allowing the creation of AI Agents entirely through natural language instructions without manual coding.

### Key Highlights

- **Expert Insights:** Listened to deep architectural knowledge and industry trends shifting from pure Generative AI to autonomous Agentic AI that can handle complex workflows.
- **Amazon Bedrock AgentCore & AgentCore Runtime:** Analyzed why enterprises need AgentCore to manage the lifecycle, execution environment (runtime), and secure resource provisioning for active Agents.
- **Identity:** Learned how to manage access control and granular permissions for each Agent to guarantee data security.
- **Gateway:** Explored the convenience of Gateway architectures in routing and managing communication traffic between end-users and the swarm of AI Agents.
- **Hands-on Lab:** Successfully practiced creating a production-ready Agent in minutes—from defining its behavior to deploying and testing it on the Bedrock AgentCore platform.

### What I Learned

Through this event, I grasped the actual workflow required to bring an AI Agent idea into Production. The concept of "Vibe Coding" truly opened a new horizon: instead of manually typing logic code, I learned how to write strict **prompts and natural language instructions** so that Kiro can automatically generate a smart, ready-to-run Agent.

Furthermore, getting hands-on with the Strands Agents SDK helped me understand the architecture behind integrating multi-agent systems, including how they communicate and share context.

### Event Experience

The atmosphere at the Workshop was extremely vibrant and hands-on. The organizers prepared a seamless Lab environment. The feeling of defining an Agent entirely in plain English and then seeing it successfully deployed and interacting exactly as described was incredibly thrilling. AWS experts and speakers were always available at our tables to troubleshoot and answer questions regarding security and scaling Agent systems for large enterprises.
