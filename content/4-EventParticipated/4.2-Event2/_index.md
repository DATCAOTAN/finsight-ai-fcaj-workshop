# Event Report "FCAJ - AGENTIC AI BUILD WEEK"

**Date:** Saturday, July 25, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower  
**Role:** Attendee  

### Purpose of the Event

- To showcase and summarize the innovative projects developed during the Agentic AI Hackathon.
- To provide an opportunity for networking and learning from international expert and top-performing teams.

### Event Highlights

- **Expert Insights:** Gained valuable knowledge and deep insights on AI from a Russian guest speaker.
- **Project Pitching:** Teams presented detailed showcases of their projects, demonstrating the Agentic AI solutions they built throughout the Hackathon.
- **Q&A and Feedback:** Interactive sessions that clarified technical challenges and real-world project implementation processes.

### Key Takeaways

- **Software Development Process:** Realized the critical importance of thoroughly reviewing content and code before pushing to GitHub.
- **Teamwork Skills:** Understood the value of team spirit and individual responsibility required to successfully deliver a Hackathon project.
- **Soft Skills:** Learned how to cope with high-pressure situations, tight deadlines, and intense workloads typical of hackathons.

### Overall Experience

Attending the event and listening to practical experiences shared by seniors and international speakers was an incredibly valuable opportunity. Witnessing fully-fledged AI products built in a short time frame greatly inspired my own learning journey.
