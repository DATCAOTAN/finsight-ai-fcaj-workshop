After an engaging period with the **First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ)** program, I would like to share some of my personal thoughts and feedback. I hope these contributions will help the organizing committee refine and develop the program even better in upcoming seasons.

### Overall Assessment

**1. Learning and Working Environment**  
The environment provided by FCAJ was highly professional, friendly, and dynamic. Attending sessions at venues like the Bitexco office brought a great sense of excitement. Members were always open to sharing knowledge, and there was no significant barrier between speakers and participants, which made me feel confident in discussions.

**2. Support from Mentors and Organizers**  
The organizing committee and mentors did an excellent job of guiding us. Whenever we encountered technical hurdles or deployment issues (especially during the Agentic AI Build Week), the mentors were ready to assist enthusiastically. They guided us towards solutions rather than just giving away the answers. The thoughtfulness in logistics was also a massive plus.

**3. Practicality of Knowledge**  
The content delivered, ranging from Cloud Architecture design to modern AI applications, closely aligns with current technology trends. This body of knowledge not only complements university studies but also provides a solid foundation for entering the corporate environment.

**4. Opportunities for Learning & Skill Development**  
The program created numerous practical challenges (such as the Hackathon) that pushed participants out of their comfort zones. As a result, I honed my ability to work under pressure, improved my teamwork skills, and learned how to successfully pitch a complete tech project.

### Evaluation Questions

* **What were you most satisfied with during the program?**  
The enthusiasm of the high-quality speakers/mentors and the practical, hands-on challenges (as opposed to just pure theory).
* **What do you think the organizers should improve for future cohorts?**  
It would be helpful to provide some pre-reading materials before deep-dive workshops so that participants can easily keep up with the fast pace of the sessions.
* **Would you recommend this program to your friends? Why?**  
Absolutely. FCAJ is a perfect stepping stone for anyone wanting to grasp Cloud and AI trends while expanding their network with industry experts.

### Proposals & Wishes

* **Proposal:** Include more networking sessions or light team-building activities in between to give members from different teams a chance to interact and connect more.
* **Wish:** I highly hope that FCAJ will maintain its momentum and potentially expand into Advanced-level training courses for program alumni in the future.
