This Worklog presents the complete eight-week FinSight AI learning and development journey during the FCAJ program. It combines AWS learning, serverless architecture design, hands-on implementation, testing and troubleshooting, security and observability, and final documentation and release preparation.

**Internship period:** 22 June 2026 – 15 August 2026

| Week | Period | Main focus |
|---|---|---|
| [Week 1](1.1-week1/) | 22–28 Jun 2026 | Project orientation and serverless foundation |
| [Week 2](1.2-week2/) | 29 Jun–05 Jul 2026 | Secure upload and document management |
| [Week 3](1.3-week3/) | 06–12 Jul 2026 | Event-driven processing and PDF extraction |
| [Week 4](1.4-week4/) | 13–19 Jul 2026 | Workflow orchestration, security, and observability |
| [Week 5](1.5-week5/) | 20–26 Jul 2026 | Cognito authentication and frontend integration |
| [Week 6](1.6-week6/) | 27 Jul–02 Aug 2026 | Structured financial analysis and provider abstraction |
| [Week 7](1.7-week7/) | 03–09 Aug 2026 | Registration, failure recovery, and Gemini integration |
| [Week 8](1.8-week8/) | 10–15 Aug 2026 | Final validation, documentation, and release |

Across the eight weeks, each learning topic was connected to a practical FinSight AI activity. The progression moved from defining a secure AWS architecture to implementing document processing, integrating a protected frontend and external development analysis provider, validating the complete system, and preparing the project for review and demonstration.
