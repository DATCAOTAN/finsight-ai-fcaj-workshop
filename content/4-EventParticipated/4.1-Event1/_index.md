# Event Report "FCAJ - CLOUD ARCHITECT"

**Date:** Saturday, June 20, 2026  
**Location:** 26th Floor, Bitexco Financial Tower  
**Role:** Attendee  

### Purpose of the Event

- To encourage learning and knowledge exchange on Cloud Computing.
- To provide a practical playground for individuals to practice system architecture design thinking.

### Event Highlights

- **Interactive Competition Format:** The event was structured as a competition between two teams, taking turns answering sets of questions ranging from easy to difficult.
- **Knowledge Focus:** The questions centered around Cloud technologies, cloud computing services, and core system architecture design concepts.
- **Atmosphere:** A balanced mix of energetic competition and an open environment for sharing professional knowledge.

### Key Takeaways

- **Architectural Thinking:** Reviewed and absorbed practical knowledge on Cloud and system design through situational questions.
- **Soft Skills:** Enhanced teamwork spirit and responsibility when collaborating with members.
- **Learning Attitude:** Sparked a proactive learning spirit and the drive to continuously expand personal knowledge.

### Overall Experience

The event was an engaging experience that combined the competitive nature of a contest with a collaborative space for knowledge sharing. This positive environment helped me realize the importance of continuous learning in the technology field.
